How it works

1. Publish or search

If you have a free room, publish it with photographs, the rent and the date it becomes available. If you are looking for one, search by neighborhood, price and date.

2. Apply

Send the host a short presentation with the date you want to move in and how long you plan to stay. The host reads every application and answers.

3. Visit and move in

The host invites you to see the house. After the visit you can leave a review, and the host chooses the housemate.
